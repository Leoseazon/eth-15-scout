## Corrected verified defects

1. **Protective-exit/Kraken coupling (critical).** `cloud/src/v2.js`: existing open paper positions now evaluate quote/depth, the >40% regular stop strictly inside six minutes, the 10% honey-pot stop and the 96c profit/hold policy **before** missing 1m/5m Kraken indicators prohibit new entries. Discretionary multi-candle structural exits still require fresh Kraken indicators. No fictitious fill may be claimed without a usable Kalshi bid and sufficient bid-side depth. Explicit deduplicated `EXIT RISK` and `EXIT FEED RECOVERED` events record missing quote, insufficient depth or wrong contract.
2. **Kalshi quote lost when a Kraken call fails (critical).** `cloud/src/worker.js`, `cloud/src/feed-health.js`: independent `Promise.allSettled` requests allow a valid, timestamp-checked Kalshi market to remain available to the exit engine when Kraken ticker, 5m, 1m or the HTTP request fails. No cached quote is substituted when the Kalshi quote itself fails its existing 90-second server-retrieval-age rule. This does **not** guarantee executability, because public REST quotes can themselves be stale or change between 15-second paper polling intervals.
3. **Opaque provider errors (operational).** The latest status includes per-source `feedChecks` with success/error and check time for Kalshi, Kraken ticker, Kraken 5m, Kraken 1m, plus Kalshi retrieval age when available. Valid 5m but invalid 1m no longer appears as an entirely healthy feed. An old market chart snapshot is cleared when the current market-data set is incomplete.
4. **Nonessential macro refresh delaying an open position (operational).** Hourly Kraken/BTC regime requests are skipped while a paper position is open, prioritizing its next bid/stop assessment. Regime information is an entry context, not an official settlement index.
5. **Synchronous fetch failure hardening.** Even a synchronously thrown fetch error is isolated and logged without discarding successful peer responses.

- **Important unresolved risks:** A REST bid from a quote retrieved as long as 90 seconds earlier may not be executable at that price; the existing cutoff was preserved to avoid an untested change to trading frequency. A 15-second poll may gap far past an exit trigger. An upstream cached response can have a recent proxy timestamp while its market bid is older. Full integration/production health and realized profitability remain unverified.

These require controlled Phase 2 shadow testing or a separately authorized rule release, not a covert safety update.
