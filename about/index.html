03 / THE PRINCIPLES

Ambitious research.
Careful claims.

Prediction markets can reprice quickly. Scout's value will be determined by repeatable evidence—not an exciting single trade.

◇

Find the price discrepancy

Study contracts that may be priced differently from the developing underlying move, without assuming a discount is automatically a bargain.

⌁

Respect the market data

Kalshi quotes and official settlement are distinct from Kraken spot and technical signals. Incomplete or stale information should not be turned into certainty.

◷

Allow a deliberate PASS

Not every contract deserves an entry. Skipping poor-quality opportunities is part of the methodology, not a missed obligation.

↺

Let the evidence refine the system

Review both strong winners and painful reversals before changing exit rules. A backtest is a hypothesis, not a guarantee of future performance.

04 / QUESTIONS

Curious about Scout?

A quick introduction for anyone following the experiment.

Does Scout trade real money? +

No. The version described here is a paper-only research and simulated trading system. Dashboard quotes and simulated entries or exits do not constitute verified real-world order fills.

Can visitors access the trading dashboard? +

No. This public page has an Owner sign in link to the existing protected V4 dashboard, but the link grants no access. Visitors cannot see private results without independently authenticating with the existing V4 credentials. No account data, credentials or private API calls are embedded here.

Does the bot guarantee profitable predictions? +

No. The goal is to test whether an evidence-based process can find worthwhile opportunities over a large sample. Short-duration prediction markets are risky; favorable individual results do not establish a reliable edge.

What happens when the data is unreliable? +

The private engine uses freshness and safety checks. If required entry evidence is invalid, new paper entries are blocked rather than made using invented data. Individual feed interruptions are evaluated during research reviews.

What is the longer-term objective? +

Build a more disciplined, explainable research process that can compare strategies, analyze decisions and learn from verified observations without confusing simulations with live performance.
